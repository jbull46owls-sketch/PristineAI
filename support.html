Common questions

How does the skin analysis work?

You take a photo of your face in good light. The photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to our AI provider, which returns a plain-language read across hydration, oiliness, texture, evenness, and redness, plus product suggestions. Anything that looks like it needs a professional is flagged for a dermatologist rather than assessed. PristineAI is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Your photo is used to generate your result and is held only in memory on our server for the length of the request. We do not save photos to our databases or logs, and photos are never synced between devices. A copy stays on your device only if you turn on photo retention, and your profile picture never leaves your device. Our analytics and crash-reporting provider never receives your photos. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data or my account?

In the app, open Settings.

Delete all my data erases your profile, routines, assessments, activity history, and any photos stored on the device, from both the device and our servers.

Delete account removes your account and the records associated with it. You can also delete individual scans from the progress view. If you need help, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

How do I turn off analytics?

Open Settings and turn off product analytics. The app then stops sending analytics events. Crash and error reports are not covered by this toggle, because they contain diagnostic information rather than your content and we need them to keep the app working.

I can’t sign in, or my code didn’t arrive.

Sign-in codes expire after a short time and can land in spam. Request a new one from the sign-in screen. If you sign in with a username, make sure you are entering it exactly, or use your email address instead. If you are still stuck, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address on your account.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account and ask for a copy of your data. We may ask you to confirm a detail to verify the request.

Does PristineAI cost anything?

The app is free to use. Product suggestions may include affiliate links, and we may earn a commission if you buy through one, at no extra cost to you. Any purchase is made on the retailer’s own site.

Suggestions are generated automatically and are not a substitute for professional advice. Check the ingredients against any allergies or sensitivities, patch-test new products, and follow the label.

1. Overview

PristineAI (“PristineAI,” “we,” “us”) is a mobile app that analyzes a photo of your face and returns a skincare assessment, product suggestions, and a personalized routine. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It is operated by PristineAI.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app. This policy works alongside the permission prompts and consent screens shown inside the app; where the app asks you separately to allow something (for example, keeping a photo on your device), that choice controls.

2. Health information and photos, in brief

  • Photos of your face are sent, over an encrypted connection, to our processing server and then to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate your assessment. Our server holds the image only in memory for the length of the request. We do not save your photos to our databases or logs, and photos are never synced between devices.
  • A copy of a face photo is kept on your device only if you turn on photo retention in the app. Your profile picture stays on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Some of what you tell us (skin concerns, allergies, whether you use a prescription treatment) and what the app produces (an assessment, a prompt to see a dermatologist) is health-related. We use it only to run the app’s features and to sync your data. We do not sell it and do not use it for advertising.
  • PristineAI is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or medical advice.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Account information. When you create an account: your email address and a password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider). Optionally, a username and a display name. You can use core features as a guest without an account.
  • Intake answers. Skin type; skin concerns (for example acne, redness, dryness); sensitivities and allergies you type in as free text; active ingredients or products you currently use, including whether you use a prescription treatment; budget preference; how much time you want a routine to take; and optional context such as age range, climate, and sun exposure.
  • Photos. Photos of your face that you capture or select for analysis; photos of product packaging you choose to scan; and an optional profile picture.
  • Routine and activity you create. The routine you build and edit, products you mark as owned, your daily check-offs and streak history, saved routines, and your notification preferences.
  • Communications. If you email us for support, we receive your message and email address.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Identifiers. The first time you open the app, an anonymous user ID is created so your data has an owner even before you register. If you create an account, that becomes your account ID. Our analytics provider also assigns a per-installation identifier.
  • Usage data. Events such as which screens you view, starting an analysis, checking off a routine step, editing your routine, opening a product link, and completing onboarding; and non-identifying counts such as how many questions you answered or how long an analysis took. These events do not include your photos, your free-text answers, or your email address.
  • Device and diagnostic data. App version, device type, operating system and version, language, and crash and error reports (including stack traces and diagnostic logs). Our analytics provider derives an approximate location (such as city or region) from your IP address.

We do not collect: advertising identifiers (no IDFA); precise or GPS location; your contacts, calendar, or messages; your photo library beyond photos you choose; or data from Apple Health or other health apps.

4. How we use information

  • To generate the assessment, product suggestions, and routine you ask for.
  • To store your routine and progress and sync them across your devices.
  • To schedule the routine reminders you enable (these are delivered locally by your device).
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and debug the app, and to prevent abuse and enforce request limits.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can improve it.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

5. How your photos are handled

  • In transit. Face photos and product-label photos are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection to our processing server, which forwards them to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to produce your result.
  • On our servers. The processing server holds an image only in memory, only for the duration of the request, and then discards it. Photos are not written to our databases, our logs, or backups.
  • On your device. A face photo is saved on your device only if you grant photo retention, which is a separate choice from the camera permission. Retained photos never leave your device through sync, and there is no photo field in our synced database. Product-label photos are stored on your device only. Your profile picture is stored on your device only and is never uploaded.
  • Deletion. You can delete a stored photo by deleting the related scan, by using “Delete all my data,” or by removing a product or your profile picture in the app.
  • No analytics or advertising access. Our analytics and crash-reporting provider never receives your photos.
  • Model training. We do not use your photos to train AI models, and we send them to our AI provider only to return your result. That provider’s handling and retention of API inputs are governed by its own terms (see section 7).

6. Health-related information

Depending on what you enter, some information may be considered health-related: your skin concerns, allergies and sensitivities, use of a prescription treatment, and the assessment the app produces (including a prompt to see a dermatologist when the app detects something it will not assess). We use this information only to provide the app’s features and to sync your data across your devices. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising or profiling unrelated to the app, and do not disclose it except to the service providers listed in section 7. Where your jurisdiction requires your explicit consent to process this kind of data, we rely on the consent you give in the app.

PristineAI is not a medical device. It describes what is visible in a photo and suggests over-the-counter products; it does not diagnose conditions and is not a substitute for professional care.

8. Analytics and your choices

  • Product analytics are on by default. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings. When off, the app stops sending product-analytics events.
  • Crash and error reporting is not covered by that toggle. These reports carry diagnostic information such as a stack trace rather than your content, and we need them to keep the app working. We remove file paths from error messages before they are sent.
  • Photos. You can decline photo retention and still use the analysis feature.
  • Notifications. You can turn routine reminders on or off in Settings or in your device settings.
  • Deletion. You can delete individual scans, delete all of your data, or delete your account from within the app (see section 11).

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and app data (profile, routines, assessment text, activity logs) is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Photos are not retained on our servers. Copies on your device remain until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics and diagnostic data is retained by our analytics provider for our configured retention period and then deleted or aggregated.
  • Backups. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period after deletion before being overwritten.
  • We may retain limited records longer where required for legal, accounting, or security purposes.

10. How we protect information

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all network requests; row-level security so each account can reach only its own data; storage of session tokens in your device’s secure keystore (Keychain or Keystore); and access controls on our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can:

  • edit your profile and routine in the app;
  • turn off product analytics in Settings;
  • use “Delete all my data” to erase your app data from your device and our servers;
  • use “Delete account” to remove your account and associated records;
  • email us at jbull46owls@gmail.com to make a request or ask a question.

We may need to verify your request, usually by confirming control of the account email address. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain why.

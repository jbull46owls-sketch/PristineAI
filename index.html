How it works

Take a photo

Point the camera at your face in good light. The photo is sent over an encrypted connection for analysis and is not stored on our servers.

Get your assessment

You get a read across hydration, oiliness, texture, evenness and redness, in everyday language. Anything that looks like it needs a professional gets pointed to a dermatologist, not assessed.

Follow a routine

Matched product suggestions become a morning and evening routine with reminders and check-offs, so you can tell whether it is working.

PristineAI is not a medical device. It describes what is visible in a photo and suggests over-the-counter products. It does not diagnose conditions and is not a substitute for advice from a doctor or a dermatologist. If you have a concern about your skin, see a professional.

Your photos and data

Photos of your face are used to generate your assessment and are not kept on our servers. A copy stays on your device only if you turn that on, and it is never uploaded through sync. Your profile picture never leaves your device.

Your account and routine data are stored so they can sync across your devices. Product analytics can be switched off in the app’s settings. You can delete individual scans, delete all of your data, or delete your account from within the app.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy, including every service that processes your data and how to exercise your rights.
